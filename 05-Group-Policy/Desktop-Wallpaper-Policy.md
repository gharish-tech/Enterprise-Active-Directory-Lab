# Desktop Wallpaper GPO

## Objective

Configure a Group Policy Object to apply a standard desktop wallpaper to domain users.

## Steps

1. Created a wallpaper folder.
2. Created a new GPO named "Desktop Wallpaper Policy".
3. Configured the Desktop Wallpaper setting.
4. Linked the GPO to the HR OU.
5. Updated Group Policy on CLIENT01.

## Result

The wallpaper policy was successfully applied to users in the HR OU.
