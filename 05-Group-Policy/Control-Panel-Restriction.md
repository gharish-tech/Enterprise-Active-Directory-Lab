# Control Panel Restriction

## Objective

Prevent HR users from accessing Control Panel.

## Configuration

User Configuration

Administrative Templates

Control Panel

Prohibit access to Control Panel and PC settings

Enabled

## Testing

Applied policy.

Logged into CLIENT01 as HR user.

Verified that Control Panel was blocked.

Logged in as Finance user.

Verified Control Panel was accessible.

## Result

The restriction applied only to the HR OU.
